# Lens & Learn

[![Made with pollinations.ai](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pollinations/pollinations/main/packages/ui/src/brand/badge-made-with.svg)](https://pollinations.ai/?ref=badge)

Mobile-friendly camera learning app: point your camera at anything (or upload a photo) to learn what it is, explore science facts or multilingual vocabulary, and listen to spoken explanations via Pollinations vision and audio models.

- **Live App:** https://30behesht.github.io/pollinations-lens-learn/
